PRIVACY FIRST

Privacy

Port Inspector is designed around local execution and user-controlled sharing.

Local diagnostics

Port diagnostics run directly from your device against the targets you select. Port Inspector does not require an OpsHome account and does not upload scan targets or results to an OpsHome backend.

Stored information

Responsive scan results and report history are stored in the app’s private application container. You can select a history retention period and remove reports from the app.

Exports

CSV and PDF reports leave the app only when you initiate an export and choose a destination through the iOS share sheet.

Permissions and network traffic

The app uses network access to contact targets included in a diagnostic you start. Only inspect systems and networks you own or are authorized to test.

Third-party services

The core diagnostic workflow does not require advertising, analytics, or an external scan-processing service.

Contact

Questions may be sent to support@opshome.run.
